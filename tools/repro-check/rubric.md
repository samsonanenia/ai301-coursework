# Rubric: is this reproduction package ready to post?

## Checks

| Check | Evidence | Pass condition | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| environment-target-match | The issue context and repo-facts block read against the repro report's environment record. | Pass when the environment record identifies the versions, platform, configuration, installation or build source, and other state that materially affect this issue. Require only environment details relevant to the reported behavior: a repository revision or checkout state is needed when reproduction depends on a source checkout, but a released package, binary, CDN build, or other identified artifact may be sufficient instead. Any meaningful difference from the issue's stated target environment must be called out. | required |
| steps-replayable | The issue's stated preconditions read against the repro report's setup, commands, inputs, fixtures, and ordered reproduction steps. | Pass when a stranger starting from the recorded environment can reach the same trigger by following the supplied commands and actions without inventing a missing prerequisite, value, file, input, or manual action. Exact issue-relevant inputs must be present or unambiguously referenced. | required |
| behavior-target-match | The output excerpts, logs, screenshots, error text, or other artifacts read directly against the trigger and behavior described in the issue context. | Pass when a successful reproduction demonstrates the issue's specific reported behavior from the intended trigger. For a cannot-reproduce result, pass when the evidence shows a concrete attempt aimed at the reported scenario and records the observed result, while explicitly identifying any issue-relevant trigger condition the contributor could not recreate. An unrelated workflow, adjacent error, different bug, unsupported paraphrase, or negative result that hides a material trigger difference does not pass. | required |
| outcome-evidence-aligned | The repro report's stated result and limitations read against its steps and artifacts. | Pass when the report says only what the evidence supports: reproduced, partially reproduced, or could not reproduce. Differences, uncertainty, and limitations that affect the conclusion must be stated. An honestly evidenced cannot-reproduce passes; an unsupported claim of reproduction, root cause, or certainty fails. | required |
| claim-specific-and-bounded | The claim comment read against the issue title, description, and relevant issue-thread context. | Pass when the claim identifies the specific issue behavior or area being investigated, states what the contributor will investigate next, and promises to report the result. It must not claim reproduction work that has not yet been performed or promise a fix, outcome, or delivery date. | required |
| repo-conventions | The repo-facts block plus the repository's CONTRIBUTING file, issue/comment templates, and stated policies read against the claim and repro comments. | Pass when the comments comply with every explicit repository requirement that applies, including any required AI-assistance disclosure. Under Path Review house rules, another student's claim or repro does not block this contributor, but the contributor must post their own claim and their own independently evidenced repro rather than piggybacking on another comment. | required |

## Verdict rule

For a full reproduction package, accept only when every required check passes. Any failed or unclear required check makes the verdict reject. Unclear counts as fail. Preferred checks, if any are added later, never change the verdict.

In live claim-only grading, checks whose named evidence does not exist until reproduction are not yet applicable and do not block the claim. Every required check that is applicable to the claim must pass.
